About Us

Mary Mother of Mercy Pre & Primary School, established in 2011, is administered by the Sisters of the Holy Spirit under the Archdiocese of Dar es Salaam. Starting as a Pre-Primary school for children aged 3-5, it expanded to Primary level in 2014 with a new campus built in 2015. Our modern facilities include classrooms, a computer lab, library, and conference room.

Mission

To uplift and integrate pupils into a bright future providing quality education, discipline, and moral values.

Vision

To build a society based on positive cultural, moral, and spiritual values for a peaceful and prosperous world.

Objectives

To provide quality education and develop pupils' intellectual, physical, and spiritual capabilities while fostering moral values and responsible citizenship.

Our Curriculum

We offer a comprehensive curriculum from Grade I to VII, following Tanzanian National Education Standards, designed to build strong foundations in core subjects while nurturing creativity and critical thinking.

Pre-Primary Education

Ages 3-5: Play-based learning focusing on early literacy, numeracy, and social skills in a nurturing environment.

Primary Education

Standards I-VII: Enhanced curriculum with English, Mathematics, Science, and Social Studies, preparing students for secondary education.

Computer Studies

Hands-on digital literacy in our computer lab, teaching basic computing, typing, and digital skills.

Religious Education

Catholic education fostering moral values, character development, and spiritual growth.
